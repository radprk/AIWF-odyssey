# Dispute Resolution Process

## Overview
This document outlines the step-by-step process for handling customer disputes on transactions, fees, or account issues.

## Types of Disputes

### 1. Unauthorized Transaction Dispute
**Timeline:** Must be reported within 60 days of statement date
**Resolution Time:** 10 business days (provisional credit may be issued within 5 days)

### 2. Billing Error Dispute
**Timeline:** Must be reported within 60 days
**Resolution Time:** 5-10 business days

### 3. Fee Dispute
**Timeline:** No strict deadline, but sooner is better
**Resolution Time:** Same day to 3 business days

## Dispute Process Steps

### Step 1: Gather Information
Collect from customer:
- Transaction date
- Transaction amount
- Merchant name (if applicable)
- Reason for dispute
- Any supporting documentation

### Step 2: Verify Customer Identity
- Confirm last 4 of SSN
- Verify registered phone number or email
- Security questions if needed

### Step 3: Document the Dispute
Create dispute case with:
- Case ID
- Customer ID
- Transaction ID
- Dispute category
- Customer's statement
- Agent notes

### Step 4: Determine Resolution Path

#### For Fee Disputes:
1. Check customer history (first-time vs repeat)
2. Review fee waiver eligibility
3. L1 can waive up to $50 in fees per year per customer
4. Above $50 requires L2 approval
5. Above $100 requires supervisor approval

#### For Unauthorized Transactions:
1. Immediately freeze affected card/account
2. Issue provisional credit if amount > $50
3. Escalate to fraud team
4. Document for Regulation E compliance

#### For Billing Errors:
1. Research transaction details
2. Contact merchant if needed
3. Issue correction or credit
4. Send written confirmation

### Step 5: Communicate Resolution
- Explain the outcome clearly
- Provide timeline for any pending actions
- Send written confirmation within 3 business days
- Document customer acknowledgment

## Escalation Triggers
Escalate to L2 or supervisor if:
- Customer requests supervisor
- Dispute amount exceeds $500
- Potential fraud involvement
- Regulatory compliance concern
- Customer has had 3+ disputes in 90 days
- Customer threatens legal action or regulatory complaint

## Fee Waiver Authority

| Role | Single Waiver Limit | Annual Customer Limit |
|------|--------------------|-----------------------|
| L1 Agent | $35 | $50 |
| L2 Agent | $100 | $150 |
| Supervisor | $250 | $500 |
| Manager | Unlimited | Unlimited |

## Regulatory Requirements

### Regulation E (Electronic Fund Transfers)
- Provisional credit within 10 business days
- Investigation complete within 45 days (90 for new accounts)
- Written notice of findings required

### Fair Credit Billing Act
- Acknowledge dispute within 30 days
- Resolve within 2 billing cycles (max 90 days)
- Cannot report as delinquent during investigation

## Documentation Requirements
All disputes must include:
- Date and time of customer contact
- Agent ID
- Full customer statement
- Research performed
- Resolution provided
- Follow-up actions scheduled
