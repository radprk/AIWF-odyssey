Q: How do I update my insurance information?
A: Log in to your patient portal, go to “Insurance & Billing,” and upload your updated insurance card. Updates are typically verified within 2–3 business days.

Q: How do I get a copy of my medical records?
A: Request records through the patient portal under “Health Records” or contact medical records. Requests are usually processed within 5–10 business days.

Q: Why was my claim denied?
A: Common reasons include missing documentation, coding mismatches, or services not covered by your plan. Review the explanation of benefits and contact billing for details.

Q: How do I pay a medical bill?
A: You can pay online via the patient portal, by phone, or by mail. Payment plans may be available through billing.
